Paperhanger Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Paperhanger is a wallpaper manager for the Mac. It is built so that your pictures and everything you do with them stay with you.

What Paperhanger collects

Nothing. Paperhanger does not collect, store or send any personal information, usage data, analytics or crash reports to its developer or to anyone else. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Your pictures and library

The pictures you import, and their tags, favorites, sets and playlists, are kept on your Mac and in Paperhanger's folder in your own iCloud account, so that your library is the same on all your Macs. iCloud storage is provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot see your iCloud data.

AI search

Searching by description and tag suggestions use an AI model that runs entirely on your Mac. Your pictures and search words are never sent to any server.

Network connections

Apart from iCloud syncing, which macOS handles, Paperhanger makes no network connections.

TestFlight

If you test a beta through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share information such as crash reports and the feedback you choose to send with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. Paperhanger itself adds nothing to this.

Children

Paperhanger collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
